Napkin Math

The 585 napkins at 50 napkins a pack take 12 packs, the last pack holding 35 napkins.

Boundary: arithmetic only Napkin Math counts an event's napkins with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the packs the napkins take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a napkin, and the minutes the folding takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your napkins, your packs or your folding table and does not know whether the napkins will really arrive uncreased, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a pack is worth its price or a napkin is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 585 napkins, 50 napkins a pack, 80 napkins a week, $6.40 a pack and 12 napkins a minute.
