Napkin Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's napkins with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the packs the napkins take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a napkin, and the minutes the folding takes. It uses no named constants. The napkins, the pack size, the weekly use, the pack price and the folding speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your napkins, your packs or your folding table and does not know whether the napkins will really arrive uncreased, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a pack is worth its price or a napkin is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The packs truth

Packs: the napkins divided by the napkins a pack, rounded up, with what the last pack holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the napkins divided by the napkins used a week, with the napkins left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the packs times the pack price, to the cent, and spread over the napkins.

The time truth

Time: the napkins divided by the napkins napkined a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.

Napkin Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
